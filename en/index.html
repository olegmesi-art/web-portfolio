The essentials

Common
questions.

How much does a website cost?+

A landing page starts at UAH 8,000, a company website at UAH 14,000 and a website with online booking at UAH 18,000. After a short brief, we fix the exact price and scope with no hidden charges.

How long does it take?+

We usually show the first design direction within 2–3 working days. A landing page takes approximately 5–7 days, a business website 7–14 days and a more complex project with integrations from 10 working days.

Can you add online booking?+

Yes. We can connect EasyWeek, SimplyBook, Fresha or another platform. The service account and subscription remain fully owned by your business.

What do you need from me to start?+

Tell us about your business and share your logo, photos, services and contact details. If anything is missing, we will help you plan what to prepare.

Will the website work on mobile?+

Yes. Every project is optimised for phones, tablets and desktops, then thoroughly tested before launch.

Who owns the website and access?+

When the project is complete, you receive all files and access to the domain, hosting, analytics and connected services. The website fully belongs to your business.

Who pays for the domain, hosting and services?+

The domain, paid hosting and booking subscriptions are registered to and paid directly by the business owner. We help you choose, connect and configure everything correctly.

What happens after launch?+

We provide 14 days of technical support after publication, fix any minor issues and explain how to use the connected services.
